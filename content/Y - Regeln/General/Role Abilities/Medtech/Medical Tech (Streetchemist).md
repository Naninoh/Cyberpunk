For every point **allocated to Medical Tech (Streetchemist), adds 1 point in the Medical Tech Skill** (up to a maximum of 10). You can only put a maximum of 5 points into this specialty. 

Once you put your first point into *Streetchemist* you can only choose one of either of the other Medical Tech specialities. If you already allocated points to both [[Medical Tech (Pharmaceuticals)]] and [[Medical Tech (Cryosystem Operation)]] you can no longer choose to add points to *Streetchemist*.

> [!Warning] Writing drugs down
> This Mechanic can bring with it a lot of additional overhead. To reduce the amount of space these Drugs take up in your Inventory, It is recommended that for [[#Unnamed Drugs]] and [[#Rapid Development Drugs]], you use the following naming convention: 
> 
> <font color="#ff0000"> **"Method":"Drug Type"/"Primary Effect Number"/"Secondary Effect Number"/"Duration"**</font>
> 
>**<font color="#ff0000">Methods</font>:** "U" = Unnamed Drug | "RD" = Rapid Development Drug
>
>**<font color="#ff0000">Drug Types</font>:** "SD" = Street Drug | "LD" = Lab Drug
>
>**<font color="#ff0000">Primary Effect Number</font>:** The Number rolled on the Primary Effect Roll
>
>**<font color="#da5f59">Secondary Effect Number:</font>** The Number rolled on the Secondary Effect Roll
>
>So an Rapid Development Lab Drug with effect 11 and 22, that last for 4 hours would look something like this:
>
><font color="#ff0000">**RD:LD/11/22/4hrs**</font>
>
>This can of course also be used for Researched Drugs, but for those more frequently used drugs, it can be beneficial to have their effects written down somewhere.
>
> *(If you are using the Cyberpunk Companion App you can add the Primary and Addiction Effects of a commonly used drug as a gear for ease of use.)*

This section holds all the Keywords used in the [[#Street Drugs]] and [[#Lab Drugs]] tables.

### Addictive
> [!example] Addictive
> Even if the drug isn't addictive, this effect makes it so. This only applies to the effects that have been added to the secondary effect, the original effect is treated as [[Medical Tech (Streetchemist)#One-Time|One-Time]].
> 
>Add to the secondary effect: <font color="#da5f59">*<font color="#da5f59">"If the user wasn't addicted to this Drug, they are now."</font></font>* Everything written under this describes the effects of the addiction. 
>
>*If the side effect of this drug has no DV to beat, the value is set to **DV15** to avoid the addiction.*

### Elusive
> [!example] Elusive
> This drug can not be researched. (It can not be made into a named replicable drug.)
> 
> If the Drug has a side effect that would result in an addiction reduce the DV by 4.

### Instant
> [!example] Instant
> This drug has no duration. You don't need to roll for its duration on the table. Directly resolve the secondary effect after the primary effect.
> 
> *If the rare case the effect had a STAT increase/decrease associated with it (for example if it was added through a secondary effect), it instead now last a flat 30 minutes.*

### MT
> [!example] MT
> **MT = Medical Tech Rank**
> 
> Substitute your rank as the number in the effect. 
> 
> *This effect is determined when the drug is made. If the drug is recreated later, the new MT may be applied to it.* 

### One-Time
> [!example] One-Time
> This side effect only triggers once, even it is applied by a drug which has permanent effects through an addiction.

### Primary Drug
> [!example] Primary Drug
> This drug has no secondary effect. You don't need to roll for it on the Secondary Effect Table.
> 
> *If the Primary Effect lists a Secondary Effect to add, it gets still applied. The Standard DV to beat that effect is **DV15**.*

### Shift
> [!example] Shift
> Shift allows you to go up or down the table you've rolled on by the number of shifts you have.
