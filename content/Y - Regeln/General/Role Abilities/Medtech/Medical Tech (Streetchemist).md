> [!Warning] Writing drugs down
> This Mechanic can bring with it a lot of additional overhead. To reduce the amount of space these Drugs take up in your Inventory, It is recommended that for [[#Unnamed Drugs]] and [[#Rapid Development Drugs]], you use the following naming convention: 
> 
> 	"Method":"Drug Type"/"Primary Effect Number"/"Secondary Effect Number"/"Duration"
> 
>**Methods:** "U" = Unnamed Drug | "RD" = Rapid Development Drugs
>**Drug Types:** "SD" = Street Drug | "LD" = Lab Drug
>**Primary Effect Number:** The Number rolled on the Primary Effect Roll
>**Secondary Effect Number:** The Number rolled on the Secondary Effect Roll
>
>So an Rapid Development Lab Drug with effect 11 and 22, that last for 4 hours would look something like this:
>
>	RD:LD/11/22/4hrs
>
>This can of course also be used for Researched Drugs, but for those more frequently used drugs, it can be beneficial to have their effects written down somewhere.
>
> *(If you are using the Cyberpunk Companion App you can add the Primary and Addiction Effects of a commonly used drug as a gear for ease of use.)*

## Glossary

This section holds all the Keywords used in the [[#Street Drugs]] and [[#Lab Drugs]] tables.
### Instant
> [!example] Instant
> This drug has no duration. You don't need to roll for its duration on the table. Directly resolve the secondary effect after the primary effect.
> 
> *If the rare case the effect had a STAT increase/decrease associated with it (for example if it was added through a secondary effect), it instead now last a flat 30 minutes.*

### One-Time
> [!example] One-Time
> This side effect only triggers once, even it is applied by a drug which has permanent effects through an addiction.

### Elusive
> [!example] Elusive
> This drug can not be researched. (It can not be made into a named replicable drug.)
> 
> If the Drug has a side effect that would result in an addiction reduce the DV by 4.

### MT
> [!example] MT
> MT = Medical Tech Rank
> 
> Substitute your rank as the number in the effect. 
### Primary Drug
> [!example] Primary Drug
> This drug has no secondary effect. You don't need to roll for it on the Secondary Effect Table.
