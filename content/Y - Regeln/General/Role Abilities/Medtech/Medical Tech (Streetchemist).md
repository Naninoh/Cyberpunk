For every point **allocated to Medical Tech (Streetchemist), adds 1 point in the Medical Tech Skill** (up to a maximum of 10). You can only put a maximum of 5 points into this specialty. 

Once you put your first point into *Streetchemist* you can only choose one of either of the other Medical Tech specialities. If you already allocated points to both [[Medical Tech (Pharmaceuticals)]] and [[Medical Tech (Cryosystem Operation)]] you can no longer choose to add points to *Streetchemist*.
