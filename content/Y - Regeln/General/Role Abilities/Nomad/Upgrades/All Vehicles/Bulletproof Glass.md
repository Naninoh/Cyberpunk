> [!warning]  Bulletproof Glass:
> Instead of providing no protection, *all glass on the vehicle is now considered to be cover*: **Thin Bulletproof Glass (15 HP)**. If **upgraded** a **second time**, it becomes **Thick Bulletproof Glass (30 HP)**. 
> 
> All windows on the vehicle sustain damage individually.
> 
> *Requires [[Moto|Nomad Rank]] 1*
