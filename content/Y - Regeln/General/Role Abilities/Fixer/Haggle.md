> [!tip] Haggle
> Haggle is the ability of the Fixer to strike a deal. 
> 
> When haggling with a person, you roll **COOL + Trading Skill + Your Operator Rank + 1d10** 
> against their 
> **COOL + Trading Skill + Their Operator Rank (if they are a Fixer) + 1d10**. 
> 
> If you succeed, you can make 1 deal of your [[Operator]] Rank or lower. Only 1 Fixer deal can be made per transaction.
