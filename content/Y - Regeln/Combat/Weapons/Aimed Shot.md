> [!tip] Aimed Shot
> An aimed shot allows you to target an enemies head, hand, or leg to trigger an additional effect with your attack.
> 
To make an aimed shot you need **to call what body part** you want to hit, then **attack with any weapon and a -8** to your roll. If you succeed you gain the effect of the targeted area.  
> 
> Aimed shots can also be useful in Vehicle Combat or be triggered by narrative events. Such as shooting out a small camera from a great distance.
