These weapons are as specialized and act like [[Exotic Weapon|Exotic Weapons]], but they are truly one-of-a-kind. This can be because the way of making them has been lost to time, they are prototypes, or the way they have been made is too difficult (or crazy) to understand for it to be replicated.

These weapons often grant a great deal of power or flexability, but come with major drawbacks.

Selling them can become a real hassle, because they're either a collectors Item so obscure it is hard to find a collector for it, or because it seems useless to most, who would otherwise be willing to buy it.

A Fixer can use a DV17 [[Haggle]] check, to sell an Unique Exotic Weapon at Market Value.
