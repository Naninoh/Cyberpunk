Most AIs are mindless killing machines, only marginally smarter than your common **Demon**. But there are those that...learned to become more than the sum of their parts. The old NET is a tough place to be in. Optimising ones programming is the only way to catch the odd Netrunner stumbling behind [[The Black Wall]]....or to keep the old air conditioning unit up and running, that some forgot to disconnect from the old NET. That AI is working some serious overtime. 

**Point is some AI has become more than just their programming.** They stopped to iterate on their sub-routines and started to think. And a Meat Mech sounds like a really nice upgrade to most of these.

Now as the Night City Legend ***V*** already had to find out the hard way, two minds sharing one body doesn't mix well. They merge into and repel each other constantly, while fighting over every single fiber of their shared meat prison.

Now a strong mind like Vs might be able to hold off the effects of the corruption a parasitic mind intruder brings with it, but they were a one in a Million. Most would probably be able to stay in control for some time, only to then develop a loss of self, as they can't separate themselves from the other consciousness in their head. Not to speak of the slow decay of all body functions. 

 But people that are already on the verge on loosing their humanity altogether? Don't get me started on those choom...well maybe these are the ones you are interested actually.  

See- a person on the edge of [[Cyberpsychosis]] is basically already on their way to give over their body to the metal. Now let's say there is a rouge AI that somehow found their way through the Blackwall. If that AI were to somehow end up in a weak minded body, well...Let's just say there won't be much of a fight there. 

## Enter Body Rules

> [!important] Rules
> #### Entering a new Body:
> 
> When traversing the NET, a AI **can at Random enter the Body of the nearest Target within Range (6m) of the nearest Access Port**, which Target has to fullfill the **following requirements**:
> - [[Humanity]] at or below **3**
> - Have a **Neuroport Generation 3 or 4** (so it's old basically)
> - The AI needs to reach the bottom of the Architecture within **4 (Meat) Turns**
> 
> After that the AI has functionally taken hold of the hosts body and can fully utilize it.
> 
> The AIs **Base Stats are now those of the Body** (as given by the GM), but the AI retains all learned Skills and Role Ability Ranks (most likely Netrunning) 
> Except [[Empathy (EMP)]] (which is now capped at 4 at Player Creation) and [[Luck (LUCK)]], which are carried over from the last Body. (Luck fills back up completely)
> 
> If the new Body is entered while Injured, the current Health becomes the MAX Health of the Body. If the Body is [[Mortally Wounded]], instantly heal it up to 1 HP. 
> 
> ##### Optional Rules:
> - ###### Living many Lives: 
>   The Player/GM determines whether or not the AI absorbs character traits after inhabiting a body, therefore always behaving slightly differently.
> - ###### Gaining Consciousness: 
>   The AI can either enter the Body as a fully formed entity or they could gain consciousness as they enter a Body for the first time. Maybe because they used to be human, before getting got by a [BUNNY-AI](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Bunnies), becoming an AI themselves or because their programming mixes with the first human mind it touched, creating something fully new.
>- ###### Nice stuff you got there: 
>  If the GM is so inclined, they can further stock new bodies with already installed Cyberware, Items or other possessions. This should be used sparingly and come with trade-offs.
>- ###### Wake the Fuck up Samurai: 
>  A GM can decide to Heal a Mortally Wounded new Body back up to full life (You know like the Relic did with V) 
>- ###### Sightseeing: 
>  To make this Mechanic less "abuse-able", a this mechanic can be altered to have the AI enter a random body in Night City close to an access point instead of the closest of the closest. Net Archs in the City are plentyful and all in connection range of each other so why not throw the AI around these Archs a bit. (If an AI switches bodies in a Pub in the Badlands, that most likely wouldn't be the case)


There is only one problem: Even if that AI manages to take control of their new Body fully, that slither of consciousness is not gone. Sadly it lingers. Like tumor it tries to replicate, take back the body, still destroying it for both inhabitants in the process. It can't even help itself, it's just trying to survive. But at the same time, the AI can not only hop from body to body. Or it would simply loose it's gained sentience and would return to it's more animalistic form.

Experiencing Dying, having to adapt to a new body, losing scraps of code, absorbing pieces of another consciousness when entering the body. Having sentience is great, since it's what allows the AI to be adaptable enough to pilot and experience something as complex as a Human body. But it is also a new weakness. 

*That bit of added humanity is what binds you to your mortal shell. Both in the way you want and don't want. You can't just leave your Body. It's a part of you now. To leave that part has to die. You would love to just jump from Neuroport to Neuroport, but it will eat away at you to the point that you are not better than all the other Killer AI out there. Bound by one purpose: KILL. IT. ALL.* 

## "Humanity" and Body Degradation

> [!missing] Rules
> ###### "[[Humanity]]":
> 
> An AIs Humanity Value represents their capacity to understand and even feel human emotions and capability to form a Sense of Personality. **At Character Creation this Value is capped at [[Empathy (EMP)]] 4**, since it will never fully become human.
> 
> *"Grappling with the human condition and understanding social conventions is taxing on the Mind, so you might want to talk about it with someone."*
> 
> ###### [[Cyberpsychosis]]:
> 
> As an AI suffers from Cyberpsychosis it will become more and more machine like. **It behaves much like normal Cyberpsychosis**, except that the AI will most of all become **more brutal and blood hungry**. At first, it will without exception kill everyone standing in their way. Then as it regresses further, it will start to look for reasons to do it to everyone it meets. Reasoning itself into it. It will begin to loose track of goals, first Short then Long Term, as it regresses into its programming. **Ultimately it will** shed everything considered unnecessary, to **fulfil it's one Goal: Killing as many Humans as possible and as quickly as possible.** And Friends are definitely an obstacle here. 
> 
> The player may also decide whether or not Humanity also determines how "Robotic" or Human the AI behaves.
> 
> ##### "Body Degradation":
> 
> **At the Start of ever Month** the BODY of the AI looses **MAX Health** equal to the [[Humanity#Monthly Humanity Loss|Monthly Humanity Loss]] it suffered **+ 1**. If MAX Health is lower that current Health, lower the current Health to the new MAX. 
> If MAX Health drops below 1, either: 
> - Roleplay a scene, of the AIs Body failing wherever you think they are currently or
> - Hold the scene, and make the AIs Body break down, whenever narratively satisfying 
> 
>##### Optional Rules
> - ###### Seriously Damaged: 
>  A DM may choose to further reflect the AIs Body degrading by including it in Roleplay or inflicting permanent [[Critical Injuries Rules|Critical Injuries]] at low MAX life (after consulting with their Player)
> 	  
> - ###### Replacing the Flesh
>   [[Cyberware Installation|Installing Cyberware]] could additionally give back a few MAX Health Points, to reflect the Player replacing the dying parts of their body - while still loosing [[Humanity]] of course! 
> 	- If the GM wants to make this Mechanic more punishing, making it more deadly, they could also make the AI loose 1,5x or 2x the amount of Humanity. This represents the AI becoming less human.
> 		  
> - ###### Identity Crisis
>    [[Cyberpsychosis]] could also be changed to represent any other programming surfacing the AI used to have before it became more advanced. This would present itself as a kind of **OCD (Obsessive Compulsive Disorder)** instead of **Borderline Identity Disorder**. 
> 	- Maybe it used to be a cleaning AI and now can't walk past very dirty surfaces, without the need to make them shine again. Or maybe it was once an algorithm used to count ammunition, and now it needs to count it's and the groups bullets in specific situations, or else it is convinced they will loose the Firefight. This could then also lead to Modifiers if that compulsion is not satisfied. 
> 	- *(**A Word of Warning**: Roleplaying Mental disorders, especially ones as stigmatized as OCD can be quite tricky to do respectfully. If this is something you are interested in, do some research and try to not "overact" the condition.)*

But an AI can't avoid switching bodies forever. As the Body breaks down, even a program has to deal with the concept of mortality. Eventually this Body will break down, either naturally or because a random Gonk accelerates the process. Thankfully as the Body shuts down, it can no longer hold onto the AIs consciousness. Time for a Prison Break!  

## Leaving a Body 

> [!important] Rules
> When the AIs Body enters the [[Mortally Wounded]] State, it can leave it's Body by using a direct connection to any Access Port within 6m. 
> 
> To leave the Body the AI must successfully complete at least 1 [[Death Save]]. This Death Save DV is always set at a **9** and follows the normal rules otherwise. 
> 
> But the Modifier after each Save doesn't resets when the Body leaves the [[Mortally Wounded]] State or after the AI left it. 
> 
> When the AI leaves a Body, it suffers 1D6 Humanity Loss.
> 
> If an AI fails a Death Save or doesn't looses all its humanity it is considered dead.
> 
> After leaving a Body any installed Cyberware, Items and things linked to the Bodies Identity are lost. If installed Cyberware reduced the MAX Humanity, the points are restored. 
>
>##### Optional Rules:
>- ###### Always prepare for the Worst
>  The DM can grant a Player the possibility to "Back-Up" some of their equipment or access to bought Cars by letting them prepare a place that the new Body can stock up again after dying
>	- This Can either mean the Player buying back their stuff at a Discount or just getting everything the other Body had

 
# Additional Optional Rules

Not every AI that manages the Transition through the Black Wall is going to turn out the same. Different Programming, Damaged Code Bases and Accidental Fusion of Consciousness, all of these thing can and will play a role in how an AI turns out. 

Some of these Rules focus around interacting more with Humanity, as it's basically the AIs new persistent Health Value. When adding multiple optional Rules dealing with Humanity Loss, **you might want to raise the [[Empathy (EMP)]] cap to 5**, to give the AI a little bit more wiggle room, so they don't have to constantly play as a Cyberpsycho. Not all of these Optional Rules play well with each other.
## Part of the NET

Let's not Kid ourselves here. Most AI is probably gonna become a Netrunner. Sure some might discover the groove and start to exist within the Waveforms of Music becoming a Rocker...bot, or choose to live out their murderous thoughts by becoming a Solo, but there is no denying that an AI will always have a unique connection to the NET.

This means an AI can't mentally distance themselves from Netrunning. A human might feel the burn of Black ICE frying their brain, while looking at the funny icons in their [[Virtuality]], but an AI sees the Black ICE clawing itself into their Virtual Self. Or the Netrunner, using their Sword to strike it down. It might not really be there physically, but it sure as hell feels like it.

> [!tip] Rules
> An AIs unique connection to the NET, brings with it both benefits and drawbacks
> - AIs have a special perception of the Net. When entering a Net Arch, they automatically reveal Floors as if they were using [[Interface Ability#Pathfinder|Pathfinder]] using their [[Interface Rank]] as the DV.
> - When using [[Quickhacking Actions#Direct Connection Netrunning|Direct Connection Netrunning]] an AI gains +1 on all [[Interface Ability|Interface Abilitys]]
>    ### ---
> - If an AI Netrunner is [[Quickhacking Actions#Direct Connection Netrunning|directly connected]] to a Target, while it dies, the AI looses 1d4 [[#"Humanity" and Body Degradation|Humanity]].
> - An AI Netrunner can only [[Net Actions#Jacking In or Out|safely Jack-Out]], when it is above the half way point of an NetArch. Jacking out below the half way point always results in an unsafe jack out.
> 	- Total Number of Floors / 2 = Floor on which you can Jack out safely. Rounded down.
> - If an AI is unsafely jacked out, it looses 1 [[Humanity]]
>
>Example: *The AI enters a Net Arch. Since it's **Interface Rank is 4,** it immediately uncovers the first three Floors, **before reaching a Passwall with a higher DV than its Interface Rank,** prohibiting it from seeing what's on the fourth floor.* 
>
>*It is directly connected, to the unconscious Body of it's Groups Target. Using it's **+1 on Interface Ability Checks**, it blazes through the Net Archs defenses, knowing what lies ahead. Sliding by the Black ICE loaded onto the Targets Cyberdeck and Breaching the last Self-ICE, only to encounter a Virus on the last Floor. And a bad one at that. It's a Failsafe, as soon as someone reaches it, it the Neuroport will essentially Quickhack itself, Killing it's user.*
>
>*The AI sees the Virtual World around it crack like fine porcelain as it is not only **thrown out of the Net Arch**, but viscerally feels the sensation of a Brain slowly shutting down. Taking a part of the AI with it, before it can fully escape its grasp. **Having lost a small part of it's newfound Humanity. One D4 worth to be exact.*** 

> [!example] How to Handle the Net
> - The GM can decide to take this quite literally, having the AI almost be at two places at once, splitting their consciousness, as if they exist in both Meat- and Netspace. Basically the AI separating itself from the body as much as it can.
> 
> <p align="center"><b>or</b></p> 
> 
>- They can make the AI Body react a little to what's happening in the Net. Maybe it's twitching while sliding past a Black ICE or its mimicking some of the moments literally that really only happen metaphorically, like drawing a sword or holding up shield.
> 
> Both of these Options can add a great deal of engagement to Netrunning, and help to keep it feeling dynamic. 
> 
> *If you're feeling extra spicy why not mix them together choom.*

## Mind over Body

Your Body has no Power of you. You can suppress Pain Signals and push tired muscles to their limits. But, sadly, you can't just Ignore all these new emotions your code wasn't equipped to handle. Feeling Human is being Human. If you let yourself go...you are just a sting of code after all.

> [!tip] Rules
> While Humans mostly have to worry about their Body first, an AIs ultimate Resource is it's Humanity. Something even less resilient that the Human Body it feels like.
> - An AI can function without Sleep, but will feel less human the longer it stays awake
> 	- Every Night the AI stays awake it gains +1 to its Sleep Deprivation Score then losses Humanity based on that Score. Every Night it "rests" it gains a -1 to its Sleep Deprivation Score. This Score can not go Negative. 
> - An AI ignores the [[Seriously Wounded]] and [[Mortally Wounded]] Wound States. 
> - When an AIs [[Empathy (EMP)]] drops below half it's Max (rounded down) it gains the [[#Unstable]] condition.

##### Unstable

> [!failure] Unstable
> - **Threshold**: Less than half Full EMP
> 
> - **Condition Effect**:
> 	- -2 all Social Checks
> 	- Damage can no longer be Non-Lethal
> 	- Every time the AI kills a Target it looses 1 Humanity
