> [!warning]
> Whenever the GM is unsure whether or not a character would take [[Humanity#Mental Trauma|mental trauma]] or withstand a situation, they can ask for a **Humanity Check**. 
> 
>> **Roll 1D100:** 
>> *If you roll under your current [[humanity]]*, you are too apathetic to care and take no humanity loss.
>> 
>> *If you roll over or equal your current [[humanity]]*, you are too empathetic and loose XD6 humanity damage (amount determined by GM).
