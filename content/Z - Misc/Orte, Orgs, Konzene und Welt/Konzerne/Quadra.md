> [!example] Overview
During a short-lived classic car renaissance during the early [2020s](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/2020 "2020"), Quadra released the [Thunder-X](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Quadra_Thunder-X "Quadra Thunder-X") as a modern homage to the vintage classic cars of the mid-20th century.
>
During the 2040s, Quadra was best known for manufacturing retro stylized muscle automotive vehicles for the general public consumer-base.
>
By the 2070s, the company excelled at designing and building muscle cars, such as the [[Quadra Type-66 640 TS|Type-66]] or their iconic [[Quadra Turbo-R V-Tech|Turbo-R]].
