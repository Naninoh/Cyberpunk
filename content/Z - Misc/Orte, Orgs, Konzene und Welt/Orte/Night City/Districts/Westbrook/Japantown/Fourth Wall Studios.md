**Fourth Wall Studios** is located in [[Japantown]], in the [[Westbrook]] district of northeastern [[Night City]]

## Overview

[Fourth Wall](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Wall "Fourth Wall") owns a large film and braindance recording complex in [[Night City]] that has several stages across the premises. Most notable is Stage 11, where in 2077, recordings were being conducted for "The Passion" picture.
