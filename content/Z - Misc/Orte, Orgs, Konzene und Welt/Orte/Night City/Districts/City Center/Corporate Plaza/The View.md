> [!hint] THE VIEW
> [![The View](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/cyberpunk/images/e/ec/The_View_Database_CP2077.png/revision/latest?cb=20210603205521)](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/cyberpunk/images/e/ec/The_View_Database_CP2077.png/revision/latest?cb=20210603205521 "The View")
>A shopping mall between Corpo Plaza and [Glen](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/The_Glen "The Glen") offering lovely views of the nearby park and port. If you have the eddies for top-shelf tech, weapons, clothes and restaurants, then the View is the place for you. Find everything you need under one roof, all draped in luxury and tasteful elegance. It is considered a premiere destination for dining and luxury shopping, as such, it is frequented by Night City's rich and famous.
