## Overview

H8 wears the colors of [the Mox](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/The_Mox "The Mox") gang (pink and blue), suggesting that the numerous Joytoys and various personnel associated with the sex industry in this building are under their protection.


## Layout
### Floor 01: Ground Floor

This floor contains a few food vendors, the main entrance, and the elevator to the rest of floors.

### Floor 03: Maintenance

This floor consists of a maintenance area, some minor loot, and another elevator to the roof. It is only available during the quests [Ex-Factor](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Ex-Factor "Ex-Factor") and [Pisces](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Pisces "Pisces").

### Floor 12: Clouds

This floor contains some apartments, a number of stores, and most notably the entrance to the dollhouse Clouds. It also has some minor loot, and an elevator to the rest of floors.

- [Clouds](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Clouds "Clouds")

### Floor 55: Roof[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcyberpunk.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FMegabuilding_H8%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

This floor consists of just a few apartment locked doors, Hiromi Sato's penthouse, and the rooftop of the megabuilding.

- Apartment 8602 to 8610.
    - Apartment 8610 - [Hiromi Sato](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Hiromi_Sato "Hiromi Sato")'s penthouse.
