The **DataKrash** was a catastrophic event in which the original [NET](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/NET "NET") was mostly destroyed by the [R.A.B.I.D.S.](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/R.A.B.I.D.S. "R.A.B.I.D.S.") viruses created by [Rache Bartmoss](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Rache_Bartmoss "Rache Bartmoss"). It occurred on June 3, 2022.[^1]

## Background

Considered the most brilliant hacker in the NET, Bartmoss was a rogue [netrunner](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Netrunner "Netrunner") who went out of his way to harass and undermine the [corporations](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Corporations "Corporations"), which he viewed as the ultimate oppressors of humanity. As a result, he lived most of his life on the run with corporate bounties on his head, and resorted to hiding in a disguised cryogenic freezer with life support tech and Net access that he spent his final days in. Bartmoss created and planted the R.A.B.I.D.S. virus in the NET in September [2014](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/2014 "2014") during the release of the [Ihara-Grubb Transformation Algorithms](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Ihara-Grubb_Transformation_Algorithms "Ihara-Grubb Transformation Algorithms") [^1] as a grand act of defiance against corporate control to be triggered in the event of his death. It was designed to breach all corporate [datafortresses](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Data_fortress "Data fortress") and unleash their data onto the Net for all to access, but it ended up having a very different effect.[^2]

When the virus was released, it infected 78.2% of the Net in a matter of months.[[1]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-RED-1) Net traffic came to a grinding halt, corporations lost billions as the stock market destabilized, huge amounts of data were corrupted, and countless military-grade [artificial intelligences](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_intelligence "Artificial intelligence") were unshackled and mutated into extremely dangerous entities.[[3]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-Spellbook-3)[[5]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-AIDB-5) [[NetWatch]], unable to reverse the damage or neutralize the AIs, decided to create the [[The Black Wall|Blackwall]], a potent AI with the sole task of blocking off sections of the Net that had been overrun by rogue AIs with ICE, so that the rest of the Net could be salvaged.[[5]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-AIDB-5) Rumors emerged that NetWatch was only able to construct the Blackwall with the help of the rogue AIs, who desired to separate themselves from human observation for their own opaque reasons.[[4]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-CyberspaceDB-4)

In the DataKrash's immediate aftermath, the Net was almost unusable. To maintain operations in the short term, many corporations and governments revived the use of 20th century [punch card technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punched_card "wikipedia:Punched card") for data entry and security purposes.[[4]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-CyberspaceDB-4) Upon the completion of the Blackwall a few years after the Krash, the Net stabilized into a very different landscape. Instead of a vast, globe-spanning network of interconnected systems subject to oversight and accountability, the Net had become fragmented, with corporations and other groups setting up a multitude of private Nets that they held unfettered control over.[[3]](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/DataKrash#cite_note-Spellbook-3) NetWatch ruled over the parts of the Net that the public could access, monitoring all users' activity with no privacy and meting out harsh punishments for anyone who attempted to see what was on the other side of the Blackwall.
