A Deep Dive allows a Netrunner to directly immerse themselves in the Net and to visualize and traverse it as if it's meat space.

Depending on the Quality of the Runners Neuroport the Net might be rendered differently to them. A big high quality Neuroport direcly hooked into the runners brain would produce an experience similar to the quality of a brain dance, while cheaper, more commonly used ones would render it as a rough outline. Most people wouldn't call the second option a true deep dive.

![[DeepDiveVis.png]]

A Deep Dive also allows the Netrunner to do a Netrun over longer distances, either setting-up a couple of streets away from their target, or using another way of directly connecting to the Net Arch remotely. 

Although this generates a lot of body heat, as it overclocks the users Cyberware and stresses their bodily functions to the max, turning them into a human oven. While most Netrunners might combat that with a cooled Netrunning Suit, a scrappier Cyberpunk might just dunk a lot of ice into a bathtub and calls it a day.
