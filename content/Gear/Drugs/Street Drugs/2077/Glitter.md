> Glitter is a **highly addictive psychedelic drug** and recent arrival on Night City's streets. The new substance quickly drew Night City's full attention. **First, it's truly strong stuff, even for the booster-and-braindance glutted residents of Night City.** Second, glitter is illegal, immediately giving it the irresistible aura of "forbidden fruit." Finally: glitter kills. 
> 
> One off-dose or weak nervous system and glitter will have those neurons firing white-hot (hence the name, perhaps). Death is inevitable.

It is made and produced by the [[Tyger Claws]], and was possibly invented by James Reddington and Jin Araki, though their individual contributions to its creation are disputed between them.

Glitter is an inhaled drug that is highly addictive, and very easy to overdose on. It is made up of multiple substances: including a strong stimulant, a psychadelic similar to LSD, and nitrous oxide.
It also has a beta blocker ([atenolol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atenolol "wikipedia:Atenolol")), likely to reduce the physical side effects of the stimulant. It may also contain additional substances not currently known, but what is known is some ingredients needed to make it are nitrous oxide, atenolol, [epinephrine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epinephrine "wikipedia:Epinephrine"), and lysergic acid. The stimulant may have some chemical similarity to [cathinone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathinone "wikipedia:Cathinone").
