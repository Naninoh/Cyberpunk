
> [!Success] Effect:
> Administered by [[Medicine|Medtech]]: Willing targets fall unconscious for 4 Hours. During this Treatment on the target gains a +2 Bonus to their [[Surgery]] Check. 
> 
> If target is unwilling, they must succeed DV15 [[Resist Torture or Drugs]] check or fall unconscious for either **1 minute (20 Combat Rounds)** or until they are awoken by taking damage or by an outside party taking an Action to rouse them.
