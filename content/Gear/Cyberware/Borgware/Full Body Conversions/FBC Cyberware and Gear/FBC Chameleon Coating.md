> **Humanity Loss**: 7 (2d6)

> **<font color="#da5f59">Effect:</font>** Becoming Invisible after standing Still for a Minute
> 
> **Description:** 
> *A light-reactive nanomachine coating developed for Militech by REDACTED. This represents the forefront of personal stealth imaging technology.*
> 
> **Extra Rules:**
> External Body Cyberware. May only be installed in a Full Body Conversion. 
> 
> When the user remains still for a 1 minute (20 rounds), they can activate a cloak that hides from all visual senses, including Low Light/ Infrared/UV, unless the perceiver succeeds at a **DV17** [[Perception]] Check. Activating the cloak does not require an Action. 
> 
> The cloak is deactivated automatically if the user moves further than 2 m/yds (1 square) within 3 seconds (1 Round). A second installation reduces the amount of time the user must remain still before the cloak can be activated to 30 seconds (10 Rounds). A third installation reduces the time to 9 seconds (3 Rounds). Further installations do nothing
