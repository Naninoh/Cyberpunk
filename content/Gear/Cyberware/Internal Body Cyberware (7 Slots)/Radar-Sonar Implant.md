> **Humanity Loss**: 7 (2d6)
> **Install**: Mall
> **<font color="#da5f59">Effect:</font>** Constantly scans for Enemies within in 50m Range. When Moving Object is detected GM says where (Mini-Map essentially)  
> 
> **Description:** 
> 
> Constantly scans terrain within 50m/yds of user, including underwater, for new threats. Scan does not include anything behind cover, like the contents of a room behind a closed door.
> 
> User receives a beep from the GM along with the direction of its source whenever a new moving object is detected on the scan. Multiple installations of this option provide user no additional benefit.
