In addition to Slugs, Shotguns can also fire Shotgun Shells. You **can't make an Aimed Shot** with a Shotgun Shell. 

> [!important]
> When you fire a Shotgun Shell, you make 1 Ranged Attack (REF + Shoulder Arms + 1d10) vs. a DV13. If successful every target in front of you, within 6m (3 squares), that you can see, takes 3d6 damage if you hit. You roll damage once for all targets. 

The Defender's [[Armor|armor will reduce the damage]] you do. Individual targets with REF 8 or Higher can still choose to attempt to dodge your Shotgun Shell.
