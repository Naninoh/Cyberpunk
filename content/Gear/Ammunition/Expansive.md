> [!tip] Description: 
> **Cost:** 100eb (Premium)
> **Ammo Types Available:** Arrows, Bullets, and Slugs
> **Special Feature:** 
> When using this ammunition, whenever you cause the Foreign Object Critical Injury, the victim rolls again on the [[Critical Injuries Rules#Critical Injury Table|Critical Injury table]] until they roll a Critical Injury that isn't Foreign Object. The victim then suffers that Critical Injury as well. This second injury deals no Bonus Damage.
