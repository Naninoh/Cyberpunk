> [!tip] Description: 
> **Cost:** 500eb (Expensive)
> **Ammo Types Available:** Grenades **only**
> **Special Feature:** 
> When using this ammunition, you deal no damage with your attack. Anyone meat hit by your attack must instead attempt to beat a DV13 [[Resist Torture or Drugs]] Check. Anyone who fails is now [[Prone]] and Unconscious for 1 minute or until they are woken by taking damage, or by someone else using an Action that touches them.
