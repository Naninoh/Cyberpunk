> [!tip] Description: 
> **Cost:** 500eb (Expensive)
> **Ammo Types Available:** Arrows and Grenades **only**.
> **Special Feature:** 
> When using this ammunition, *you deal no damage with your attack*. Anyone meat hit by your attack must instead attempt to *beat a DV15 Resist Torture/Drugs Check.* Anyone who fails is dealt *3d6 damage directly to their HP*. Their armor isn't ablated because it wasn't interacted with.
