> **Description:** 
> An [[Excellent Quality]] Two-Handed Exotic [[Very Heavy Melee Weapon]].

> 
> *In the hands of a user without the correct biometric key, the blade will not vibrate, leaving it only an Excellent Quality Two-Handed Exotic Very Heavy Melee Weapon. At no additional cost, any color of laser can be installed into the hilt to refract within the crystal blade, giving it a flaring neon glow. The most popular color is red.*
