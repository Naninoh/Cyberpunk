> **Type:** [[Exotic Weapon]]
> **Cost:** 500eb (*Expensive*)
> 
> **Description:** 
> An [[Exotic Weapon|Exotic]] [[Very Heavy Pistol]]. Whenever a user fires this weapon without some form of earprotection, they suffer the [[Damaged Ear]] [[Critical Injuries Rules|Critical Injury]]. Instead of dealing damage, on a hit it forces the target to try to beat a DV15 [[Resist Torture or Drugs]]. If they fail, they suffer the Damaged Ear Critical Injury. Ammunition is costless as it runs off of easily rechargeable (1 hour) battery packs that need to be replaced after 8 shots. Extra battery packs are 50eb (Costly).
