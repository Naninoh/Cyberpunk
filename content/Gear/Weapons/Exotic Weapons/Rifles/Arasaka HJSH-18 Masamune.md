Designed in the 2050s by Arasaka. Every single one of the Masamune’s elements, down to the tiniest screws and bolts, were quality-ensured by a legion of Arasaka engineers – and you can feel it. 

Based on the [Nowaki](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Nowaki "Nowaki") from the early [21st century](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/21st_century "21st century"), the Masamune is a high-quality assault rifle designed in the [2050s](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/2050 "2050"). Like its predecessor, it fires exclusively in highly accurate, three-round bursts. The weapon has a 24-round magazine with an average reload time. In the lore, the Masumane is waterproof and can be used underwater.
