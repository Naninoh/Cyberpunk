Easy to use drug distribution platform which uses a quick burst of compressed air to force a drug through the skin. 

Allows user to use an Action to administer a single dose of a desired drug to a willing target, or try to make a [[Meele Weapon|Melee Weapon Attack]] to **administer a single dose to an unwilling target on a hit instead of dealing damage**. 

Reloading the Airhypo with a dose of your desired drug isn't an Action.
