
> [!example] Description
> 
> **Cost:** 100eb (*Premium*)
>
> **Quick Description**: An inflatable Decoy that will register as Human to [[Smart Rebuild|Smart Weapons]] and [[Tech Rebuild| Tech Weapons]] 
>
> **Description:** 
> On the battlefield, when seconds count, a moment of disorientation means a world of difference. With a thermal decoy, never before has subterfuge cost so little, or been worth so much.
> 
> *A Thermal Decoy can be set up as an Action*, and inflates immediately upon setup. Thermal decoys register as human to  [[Smart Rebuild|Smart Weapons]] and [[Tech Rebuild| Tech Weapons]]  and potentially human when viewed through Infrared Nightvision Scopes, or [[Low Light, Infrared, UV]] cyberware or goggles.
> 
> When viewed in a high pressure environment, a decoy may appear initially to be a combatant. In combat, *it enters the Initiative Queue just like a combatant.* On “their” Turn, a Thermal Decoy either mysteriously *“Holds their Action” or “Aids one of their teammates* (don’t actually give a teammate a benefit)”. 
> 
> *On the Turn of a character who the decoy is intended to deceive, the GM secretly makes a DV17 Perception Check* for them to determine if they notice the false nature of the Decoy, giving characters that are relying entirely on Low Light / Infrared or Radar/Sonar to see a -2 penalty. If a Thermal Decoy takes damage or is otherwise revealed as a decoy to all those it intended to deceive, it drops out of the Initiative Queue. Thermal decoys that take any damage are destroyed.
