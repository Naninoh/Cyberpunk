The Flathead is an experimental military grade unit for surveillance and reconnaissance missions, manufactured by [Militech](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Militech "Militech"). It is state-of-the-art, coming without a standard port and with titanium-fiber actuators. It has dynamic camouflage armor and motor impulses rivaling that of the human nervous system.

Normally, the robot would be controlled by Militech software, but it can be replaced with an alternative version, for example by [Raven Microcybernetics](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Raven_Microcybernetics "Raven Microcybernetics") and be placed on a control shard.
